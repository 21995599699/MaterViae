Privacy Policy — Mater Viæ

the Mother who shows the way

Updated November 12, 2026.

The Mater Viæ app does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.

Your chosen language, Journey progress, favorite phrases, phrase history and reminder times are stored only on your device and are deleted when you remove the app.

Notifications (phrase of the day and reminders) are optional and scheduled on the device itself; nothing is sent from a server.

The app works offline. If online features are added in the future, this policy will be updated first.
